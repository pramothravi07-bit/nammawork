WE ARE HERE FOR YOU

Help & support

Find guidance for using NammaWork.

How do I record work?

Open Work History and choose Record a shift. Select a household, date, start time, end time, and break.

How are wages estimated?

Expected wages are calculated using recorded hours and the hourly rate saved for a household.

How do I record a dispute?

Open Disputes and add a description of the concern. Keep relevant records for your reference.

Need urgent help?

For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted support organization.
